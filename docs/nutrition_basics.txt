Balanced nutrition means consuming the energy and nutrients the body needs in adequate and regular amounts.

Proteins help build and maintain muscles, tissues, and cells. Eggs, yogurt, milk, cheese, fish, chicken, meat, lentils, and chickpeas are examples of protein-containing foods.

Carbohydrates are one of the body’s main sources of energy. Whole grains, oats, bulgur, fruits, vegetables, and legumes can provide carbohydrates.

Fats provide energy and help the body absorb and use certain vitamins. Olive oil, avocado, walnuts, almonds, and hazelnuts are examples of healthy fat sources.

Vegetables and fruits contain vitamins, minerals, and fiber. Eating vegetables and fruits of different colors can increase dietary variety.

Water is necessary for regulating body temperature and maintaining normal body functions.

Nutritional needs may vary depending on a person’s age, weight, physical activity level, and health condition. People with an illness or a special health condition should consult a healthcare professional for personalized nutrition advice.
