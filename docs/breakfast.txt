A balanced breakfast can include protein, fiber-rich carbohydrates, and healthy fats.

Eggs, cheese, yogurt, and milk are protein sources that can be included in breakfast.

Oats, whole-grain bread, and fruit are examples of fiber-rich carbohydrate sources.

Walnuts, almonds, hazelnuts, and avocado can be included among healthy fat options for breakfast.

An example breakfast can consist of eggs, whole-grain bread, tomatoes, cucumbers, and yogurt.
